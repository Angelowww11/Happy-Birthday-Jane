## Prompt set

**Floral corner:** An elegant, lively, asymmetrical transparent corner arrangement of two lush blush-pink peonies and two open ivory magnolia blossoms, sage leaves and tiny yellow stamens. Premium hand-painted gouache and watercolor, crisp cut-paper edges, tactile brush texture, editorial stationery style. L-shaped cluster growing from the bottom-left toward the upper-right. Isolated with real alpha. No paper, shadow, text, or watermark.

**Peony:** A single full blooming peony with layered ruffled petals in soft pink and raspberry, a curved stem and sage leaves. High-end hand-painted gouache with subtle paper grain, playful and romantic, clear petal anatomy. Centered isolated composition, real alpha. No border, text, background, or watermark.

**Magnolia:** A graceful magnolia branch with two large creamy white blossoms, pale pink undersides, golden centers, glossy leaves, and buds. High-end hand-painted gouache and watercolor with delicate brush texture. Diagonal isolated composition, real alpha. No text, background, or watermark.

**Nailong with peony:** Edit the existing yellow plush sticker. Preserve its round yellow head, open brown eye, winking eye, smiling pink mouth, plush texture, red fuzzy scarf, white belly, limbs, and white sticker outline. Slightly change its pose so it gently holds a small pink peony bloom beside its chest. Candid collectible sticker from the same photographed plush. Real alpha, no extra character, text, background, or watermark.

**Nailong with magnolia:** Edit the existing yellow plush sticker. Preserve the same face, scarf, belly, limbs, plush texture, and white outline. Pose it playfully peeking from behind a large cream magnolia bloom with pink edges, one paw resting on the flower. Real alpha, no extra character, text, background, or watermark.

**Birthday flower garland:** Create a single transparent decorative cutout for a playful romantic birthday website. An asymmetrical crescent garland of large pale blush peonies and creamy ivory magnolia blossoms, a few butter-yellow blossom centers, delicate green leaves, tiny curved ribbons and scattered petals. Hand-painted watercolor meets die-cut sticker with soft dimensional shading and clean silhouettes. Open transparent center; no text, border, background or people. Warm white, buttery yellow, soft pink and light sage. Generated with the built-in image generation tool and optimized to transparent WebP.

Nailong artwork in the published site uses the plush cutout from the couple's own photo and its peony/magnolia edits. A separate generated dragon concept was discarded because it did not resemble Nailong.
